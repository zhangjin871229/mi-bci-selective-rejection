**Protocol amendment 1 (made before any evaluation label was read).**

- Date: 2026-10-04
- Reason: For A02 the EEGNet validation NLL (run 5, ~46 trials) increased monotonically from epoch 1 while validation accuracy rose from 0.15 to 0.48, so the v1 rule selected 1 epoch (an essentially untrained network).
- Change: epoch_selection.rule replaced by: minimum 5-epoch moving-average validation NLL over epochs >= 10; if that value is not lower than the epoch-1 value, use the epoch maximising 5-epoch moving-average validation accuracy (earliest on ties). Applies to ChannelViT and EEGNet, all subjects. v1 selections are kept in models/<S>/epochs.json (*_v1).
